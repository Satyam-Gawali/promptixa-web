Delete Your Promptixa Account

Promptixa by Satyam Studios
Last Updated: August 29, 2026

If you would like to delete your Promptixa account and associated personal information, you can submit an account deletion request using the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email from the email address associated with your Promptixa account to:

Email: satyamstudios.dev@gmail.com

Use the subject line:

Delete My Promptixa Account

In the email, clearly state that you want to delete your Promptixa account.

For security purposes, we may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request belongs to you.

What Happens After Your Request

We will review and process eligible account deletion requests within a reasonable period.

Once the request is processed, information associated with your account will be deleted where applicable.

Information That May Be Deleted

Account deletion may include deletion of personal information associated with your Promptixa account, such as:

  • Account information
  • Profile information
  • Saved content associated with your account
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Please note that deleting your Promptixa account does not automatically guarantee the removal of content you have previously submitted or published. Your submitted or published community content may remain available on Promptixa even after your account has been deleted. If you want your submitted or published content to be removed, you may need to submit a separate content removal request.

Content Removal Requests:

If you want your submitted or published community content to be removed, please contact us at satyamstudios.dev@gmail.com with details of the content you want removed.

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary for purposes such as security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

Important

Account deletion is intended to be permanent.

After your account has been deleted, you may not be able to recover your previous account information or account-related data.

If you later want to use account-based Promptixa features, you may need to create a new account.
