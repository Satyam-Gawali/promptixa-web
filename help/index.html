1. Getting Started & Overview

3 Articles
Overview

What is Promptixa? (And what it is NOT)

Promptixa is a curated and community-driven AI prompt discovery, customization, and library platform. It is designed to help you quickly discover high-quality prompts, customize them with your own details, and use them with external AI tools.

What You Can Do on Promptixa:

  • Discover AI Prompts: Browse curated prompts across multiple domains and categories.
  • Explore Feeds: Discover prompts in New, Popular, and Featured sections.
  • Customize Prompts: Fill in customizable variables/placeholders (such as <topic>, <style>, <tone>) to tailor prompts to your specific needs.
  • Unlock Prompts: Unlock locked community prompts for your session by watching a rewarded ad.
  • Copy & Export: Copy final customized prompts directly to your device clipboard.
  • Launch in External AI: One-tap transfer to ChatGPT and Google Gemini.
  • Save Favorites: Bookmark favorite prompts to your personal collection (requires sign-in).
  • Submit Prompts: Share your own creative prompts with the community and track their status.
Important: Promptixa is NOT a Chatbot
Promptixa does NOT generate conversational AI responses or act as an AI assistant inside the app. Promptixa is purely a prompt discovery and management library. To generate AI answers, you copy or send your prompts to dedicated AI services like ChatGPT or Google Gemini.

Discovery

How Prompt Sections & Feeds Work

Promptixa provides several discovery sections to help you find the right prompt quickly:

  • New: Displays the latest prompts published to Promptixa, ordered by their creation date.

3. Unlocking & Copying Prompts

2 Articles
Unlocking

How to Unlock Locked Prompts (Rewarded Video Ads)

Some prompts on Promptixa are locked for regular users. Promptixa is completely free to use — prompts are never sold, there are no subscriptions, and you never purchase individual prompts. Instead, locked prompts are unlocked via rewarded video ads.

Step-by-step: How to Unlock a Prompt

  1. 1
    Open the locked prompt to view its details.
  2. 2
    Tap the Copy Prompt button (or launch ChatGPT / Gemini).
  3. 3
    Watch the short rewarded video ad until completion.
  4. 4
    Upon successful completion of the ad, the prompt is unlocked for your current app session.
  5. 5
    The prompt text is copied to your clipboard, and you can copy or export it freely during your session.
Creator Privilege: If you are the author/creator of a prompt, you can always access, view, and copy your own submitted prompt instantly without watching an ad.
Clipboard

Copying Your Customized Prompt

When you tap Copy Prompt on an unlocked prompt, Promptixa compiles the final prompt text including any custom variable values you have filled in, and copies it directly to your device's clipboard.

You can then paste it anywhere on your device — into a text editor, notes app, messaging app, or your preferred AI tool.

4. Prompt Variables & Customization

2 Articles
Variables

How Prompt Variables and Placeholders Work

Many prompts in Promptixa are designed with placeholders enclosed in angle brackets, such as <topic>, <style>, <tone>, or <language>.

For example, a prompt might read:

"Write a <tone> article about <topic>."

When Promptixa detects these placeholders in a prompt, it automatically creates input fields under a section labeled "Customize Variables:".

How to Use Variables:

  1. 1
    Open the prompt details page.
  2. 2
    Scroll to the Customize Variables: section.
  3. 3
    Type your desired values into the variable fields (e.g., Topic: Cybersecurity, Tone: Professional).
  4. 4
    The prompt text updates immediately to reflect your input.
  5. 5
    Tap Copy Prompt (or send to ChatGPT / Gemini) to get the final customized prompt.
Reminder: This variable replacement is a prompt template customization feature. Promptixa does not generate an AI answer to the prompt — it prepares the exact text you want to submit to an AI tool.
Tips

Writing Variable Placeholders in Your Own Prompts

When you create and submit a prompt on Promptixa, you can add your own variable placeholders by enclosing short descriptive names in angle brackets:

  • <topic> — Subject of the request
  • <tone> — Voice or emotion (e.g., friendly, academic, persuasive)
  • <target_audience> — Who the output is intended for
  • <word_count> — Desired length or scope
  • <format> — Output style (e.g., table, bullet points, Markdown)

When other users open your published prompt, Promptixa will automatically generate user-friendly input fields for each variable!

5. Using with External AI (ChatGPT & Gemini)

2 Articles
ChatGPT

Using Prompts with ChatGPT & Clipboard Fallback

Promptixa makes it easy to launch your customized prompt in ChatGPT with a single tap.

What Happens When You Tap the ChatGPT Action:

  1. 1
    Your customized prompt (including any variables you filled in) is automatically copied to your device clipboard.
  2. 2
    Promptixa attempts to launch ChatGPT with the prompt text prefilled.
  3. 3
    Clipboard Fallback: Due to differences in external browsers and third-party app updates, automatic prefilling cannot be guaranteed in every environment. If the prompt does not automatically appear in ChatGPT's message box, simply tap in the input field and select Paste.
Gemini

Using Prompts with Google Gemini (App & Web)

Promptixa provides direct integration with Google Gemini for both app and web users.

How Gemini Launching Works:

  • On Android: Promptixa attempts to open the installed Google Gemini app. If the Gemini app is not installed or cannot be opened, Promptixa falls back to opening Gemini on the web.
  • Clipboard Safety: Before launching Gemini, Promptixa always copies your final customized prompt to the device clipboard.
  • Clipboard Fallback: If the prompt is not automatically inserted into the Gemini prompt box, you can simply paste the prompt from your clipboard.

6. Creating & Managing Submissions

3 Articles
Submission

How to Create and Submit a Prompt

Signed-in users can share their own high-quality prompts with the Promptixa community through the Create tab.

Required Submission Information:

  • Title: A clear, concise title describing what your prompt accomplishes.
  • Description: Brief instructions or context explaining when and how to use it.
  • Prompt Text: The complete prompt template, optionally including variable placeholders like <topic> or <tone>.
  • Category: Select the relevant category from the available list.
  • Tags: Up to 6 tags maximum to help users find your prompt in searches.
  • Preview Image: Provide an appropriate preview image when required.
Strict Tag Limit (Maximum 6 Tags):
Promptixa enforces a strict maximum of 6 tags per submission. If you try to submit more than 6 tags, your submission will fail. Always ensure your tag count is 6 or fewer.
Status

Understanding Submission Statuses (Pending, Published, Unpublished)

You can track and manage all of your submitted prompts in the My Submissions section under your Profile tab. Submissions are categorized into the following statuses:

  • All: A comprehensive view of all prompts you have submitted across all statuses.
  • Pending: Your submission has been received and is currently waiting for review by the moderation team.
  • Published: Your prompt has been approved and is currently live for other Promptixa users to discover and use.
  • Unpublished: Your prompt was approved, but has been temporarily hidden from public view by you (the creator).
Favorites

Saving & Managing Your Favorite Prompts

You can save any prompt to your personal collection by tapping the Heart (Favorite) icon on the prompt card or prompt details page.

  • Requires Google Sign-In: If you are browsing as a guest and tap Favorite, Promptixa will prompt you to sign in with Google so your collection can be securely saved.
  • Accessing Favorites: Tap the Favorites tab in the bottom navigation bar to view and manage all of your saved prompts at any time.

8. Troubleshooting & Common Issues

4 Articles
Troubleshooting

Prompt Won't Unlock (Rewarded Ad Not Playing or Loading)

If a locked prompt does not unlock after tapping Copy Prompt, try the following recommended troubleshooting steps:

  1. 1
    Check Internet Connection: Ensure your device has an active, stable internet connection.
  2. 2
    Toggle Network: Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
  3. 3
    Disable Ad Blockers / VPNs: Ad-blocking applications, custom VPNs, or private DNS settings (e.g. NextDNS, AdGuard) can block rewarded ad delivery. Temporarily pause them and retry.
  4. 4
    Wait & Retry: Ad networks occasionally experience brief inventory delays. Wait a few moments and tap the button again.
  5. 5
    Contact Support: If the issue persists across multiple prompts, email us at satyamstudios.dev@gmail.com.
Troubleshooting

ChatGPT or Google Gemini Does Not Open or Prefill

If tapping the ChatGPT or Gemini buttons does not launch the respective tool or prefill the text:

  • Use Manual Paste: Promptixa always copies the prompt to your clipboard before launching external apps. If the text does not automatically appear in the input box, simply tap and hold inside ChatGPT / Gemini and select Paste.
  • Verify App Installation: Make sure the ChatGPT or Google Gemini application (or your preferred web browser) is installed and updated on your device.
  • Check Connectivity: Ensure your device is online so external web links can load.
Troubleshooting

Prompt Submission Fails or Returns an Error

If submitting a prompt fails, please verify the following checklist:

  • Tag Limit Check: Confirm that you have added no more than 6 tags. Adding 7 or more tags will cause the submission to fail.
  • Required Fields: Ensure that Title, Description, and Prompt Text are filled in.
  • Category Selected: Verify that you have selected a valid category from the dropdown.
  • Preview Image: Ensure a preview image is selected if required for the category.
  • Sign-In State: Confirm that you are actively signed in with Google.
App Status

App Updates (Mandatory / Optional) & Maintenance Mode

App Update Notifications

  • Mandatory Updates: If your installed app version is too old or incompatible with current backend services, Promptixa will display an update-required screen. Tap Update Now to update the app from your app store.
  • Optional Updates: When a new feature or improvement is available, Promptixa may show an update prompt with options to Update Now or Later.

Maintenance Mode

During scheduled system maintenance or service upgrades, Promptixa may temporarily show a maintenance screen. You can tap Check again after a short period. If the maintenance screen continues to appear for an extended duration, please contact support.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Promptixa completely free?

Yes. Promptixa is completely free to use. There are no subscriptions, paid tiers, or purchases. Some community prompts are unlocked for your session simply by watching a short rewarded video ad.

Why isn't Promptixa answering my questions like ChatGPT?

Promptixa is not an AI chatbot or conversational assistant. Promptixa is a curated library of high-performance prompt templates. To get AI responses, customize the prompt variables inside Promptixa, copy the prompt, and paste it into ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or your favorite AI tool.

How many tags can I include when submitting a prompt?

You can include a maximum of 6 tags. Submissions with more than 6 tags will not be accepted by the system.

Can I manually switch between Light and Dark mode?

Promptixa automatically syncs with your device's system appearance (Light or Dark). To change Promptixa's appearance, change your device's system theme settings.

Do creators have to watch ads to unlock their own prompts?

No. If you created and submitted a prompt, you can always view, customize, and copy your own prompt without watching any rewarded ads.
