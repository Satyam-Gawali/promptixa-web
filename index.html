What is Promptixa? (And what it is NOT)

Promptixa is a curated and community-driven AI prompt discovery, customization, and library platform. Promptixa is not a chatbot or conversational AI assistant and does not generate AI responses inside the app. It gives you crafted, variable-customizable prompts that you can copy or send directly to external tools like ChatGPT and Google Gemini.
