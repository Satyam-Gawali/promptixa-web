01 / Product Overview

Your prompts, ready for more.

Promptixa helps creators, developers, and writers discover curated AI prompts, customize them with dynamic variables, and launch them into leading AI tools with a single tap.

Instead of rewriting complex prompts from scratch every time, Promptixa provides tested prompt architectures designed for repeatable, high-quality results across diverse creative and technical disciplines.

What Promptixa Is

Curated Discovery & Customization

A community-driven prompt library where you explore tested templates, tailor custom variables directly on screen, and launch finalized prompts into ChatGPT and Google Gemini.

What It Is Not

Promptixa Is Not a Chatbot

Promptixa does not generate AI responses inside the app and has no internal chat interface. It prepares the exact prompt instructions you take into your external AI tools.

04 / Variable Engine

Make a prompt yours.

Promptixa dynamic variables turn static text into modular prompt systems. Any placeholder wrapped in angle brackets automatically generates an interactive input field on your device.

promptixa-variable-compiler.sh LIVE SYNTAX
"Create a <visual_style> portrait of <subject> in <location> with <lighting> ."
TRANSFORM CONTEXT:

Syntax standard: <variable_name>. Creators can define any custom placeholder: <target_audience>, <brand_voice>, <code_language>, etc.
